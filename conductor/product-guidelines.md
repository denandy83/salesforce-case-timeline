# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
-   **Professional & Efficient:** All text within the component—including error messages, tooltips, and empty states—must be concise, direct, and action-oriented.
-   **Clarity over Fluff:** Avoid conversational filler. Provide the user with the exact information they need to make a decision or take the next step.
-   **Consistent Terminology:** Ensure that terms like "Internal," "External," and "System" are used consistently across all labels and filters.

## Visual Principles
-   **Information First:** The design must prioritize data density. Use a compact, line-item based layout with minimal whitespace to maximize the number of visible events without excessive scrolling.
-   **Iconography as Language:** Use a consistent set of high-contrast icons to serve as the primary visual differentiators for message types and event categories.
-   **Visual Hierarchy:** Use typography (weight and size) and subtle color cues to emphasize the most important information (e.g., the subject of an email or the timestamp) while keeping secondary data less prominent.

## Interaction & UX Patterns
-   **Non-Disruptive & Contextual:** Major actions, such as the "Quick Reply," should expand inline or open in a side panel. This ensures that the user maintains their context within the timeline while performing the action.
-   **Low Friction Filtering:** Filters should be easily accessible and provide immediate visual feedback. The UI should update instantly without full-page reloads.
-   **Persistent State:** Where possible, the component should remember the user's last-used filters or density settings to reduce the need for repetitive configuration.
