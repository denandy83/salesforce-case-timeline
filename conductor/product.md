# Product Definition

## Vision
The **Salesforce Case Timeline** is a specialized Lightning Web Component designed to revolutionize how support agents and organizational stakeholders view and interact with case history. By transforming disjointed related lists into a unified, interactive, and visually distinct chronological stream, it empowers users to understand case context instantly and take action without friction.

## Core Problem
Salesforce users, particularly in high-volume support environments, struggle to gain a rapid understanding of a case's history. Standard interfaces often require navigating multiple related lists or reading through unstructured feeds, leading to:
-   **Cognitive Load:** Difficulty in mentally reconstructing the sequence of events (emails vs. internal notes vs. system updates).
-   **Inefficiency:** Time lost switching contexts to reply to emails or update case details.
-   **Missed Context:** Critical internal discussions or system-generated milestones getting lost in the noise of external communications.

## Target Users
1.  **Customer Support Agents:** The primary power users who need to "read" a case status at a glance and respond immediately.
2.  **Service Team Leads:** Supervisors who need to audit case progression and quality without deep-diving into every record.
3.  **Cross-Functional Employees:** Non-support staff (e.g., Sales, Success) who require a quick, accurate summary of client interactions without navigating the full service console.

## Key Features
-   **Unified Chronological View:** A single timeline aggregating emails, comments, field history, and milestones.
-   **Smart Segmentation:** Visual separation of **Internal**, **External**, and **System** messages using distinct color-coding, icons, and badges to allow users to scan for specific context types instantly.
-   **Actionable Interface:**
    -   **Quick Reply:** Integrated functionality to reply directly from the timeline item in an expanded, focus-mode window, reducing clicks and context switching.
-   **Advanced Filtering:** Users can toggle visibility of specific event types (e.g., "Show only Emails" or "Hide System Messages") to focus on relevant information.
-   **Personalization:**
    -   **User Preferences:** Configuration options allowing individual users to persist their preferred view settings (density, default filters) for maximum personal efficiency.
-   **Visual Enhancements:** A modern, high-contrast design that improves upon standard Salesforce styling for better readability and information density.
